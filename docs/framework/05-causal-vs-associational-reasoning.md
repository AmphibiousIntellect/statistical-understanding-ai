# 5. Causal vs. Associational Reasoning

The capacity to distinguish description, association, prediction, explanation,
and causal inference and to identify the assumptions required for each.

Observable capabilities include recognizing confounding, selection and collider
bias, time ordering, post-treatment adjustment, target trial elements, and the
limits of adjusted observational estimates.

Candidate tests should perturb design and bias information while holding the
surface wording and numerical result nearly constant.
