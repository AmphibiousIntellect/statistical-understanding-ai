# 6. Statistical Judgment

The capacity to determine what can be concluded, what cannot be concluded, what
additional information is required, which analytical choices are defensible,
and when human statistical review is necessary.

Observable capabilities include identifying underspecification, comparing
reasonable alternatives, evaluating assumptions and sensitivity, recognizing
practical versus statistical significance, and resisting pressure to overclaim.

Evaluation should reward appropriate hesitation and information seeking, not
only answer production.
